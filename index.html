The 11:11 Approach

One team, whatever the occasion asks for.

A wedding, a stage with a headline act, a ribbon waiting to be cut, a hall of delegates, a campus fest, a tournament final — different rooms, the same discipline behind them.

We are a Bhubaneswar-based team of planners, producers and coordinators. Whether the brief is three hundred guests or three thousand, the work is the same underneath: considered timelines, generous buffers, vendors and artists who know exactly where to be, and a team that handles the unexpected before it ever reaches you.

Services

How We Can Help

Three ways to work together, shaped around how much of the event you want us to carry.

Service 01

Full-Service Management

We take the event end to end — concept, budget, vendors, permissions, and the running of the day itself.

  • Venue search & vendor curation
  • Budget structure & guest logistics
  • On-site management from setup to wrap

Service 02

Celebrity & Artist Booking

Securing the name on the poster — from local artists and Odia performers to national acts — and handling everything that comes with them.

  • Artist sourcing & negotiation
  • Contracts, riders & hospitality
  • Travel, security & green room

Service 03

Design, Stage & Production

The look and the machinery behind it — sets, staging, sound, lighting, and the crew who make it run.

  • Concept direction & palette
  • Stage, set & floral installation
  • Sound, lighting & AV production

What We Run

Every Kind of Gathering

Six kinds of event, one team. The scale changes, the standard does not.

  • Weddings & Social

    Full ceremonies and receptions, engagements, anniversaries and private family celebrations.

  • Celebrity Nights

    Live shows and private parties built around a headline act, with the artist, stage and security handled end to end.

  • Inaugurations & Launches

    Openings for hotels, restaurants, showrooms and institutions — ribbon to press coverage.

  • Corporate & Conferences

    Annual days, dealer meets, seminars and award nights, with the AV and delegate logistics that go with them.

  • College & Campus

    Cultural fests, tech fests, farewells and convocations — run to a student budget without looking like one.

  • Sports Events

    Tournaments, league days and prize ceremonies, including ground setup, scoring and the trophy moment.

Our Process

A Thoughtful Process

  1. 01

    Connect

    Tell us what the event is, who it is for, and what it has to achieve.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We build the budget and run sheet, lock the venue, vendors and artists, and clear the permissions.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    The day arrives. Our crew runs it, and you get to be a guest at your own event.

  4. 04

    Wrap

    Teardown, settlements and a closing report. The details fade; the event is what people remember.
