The 11:11 Approach

Your day should feel as beautiful as the moments you’re celebrating.

From the first conversation to the final farewell, we thoughtfully coordinate every detail so you can be fully present for the people and moments that matter most.

We are a Bhubaneswar-based team of planners and coordinators who believe a wedding day should feel unhurried. That comes from months of quiet preparation — considered timelines, generous buffers, vendors who know exactly where to be, and a team that handles the unexpected before it ever reaches you.
