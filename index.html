Our Profile & Vision

The alignment of time, place and artist.

11:11 represents the exact alignment of time, place and artist needed to create an impossible-to-replicate cultural moment. We exist to transform high-end private gatherings into extraordinary, intimate experiences through meticulous venue curation and elite talent matchmaking.

Elevenn Elevenn Archive is an experiential event agency built on a single core promise: curating high-impact, unforgettable atmospheric experiences. We do not just run events — we pioneer niche cultural movements. From intimate, high-end gatherings to large-scale concert productions, our specialty lies in connecting discerning audiences with exceptional talent.

Our core difference is that we bridge the gap between audience expectation and sonic discovery. Rather than relying solely on mainstream, repetitive headliners, we scout and bring forward India’s finest underground and breakout artists — nationally recognised talent whose sets leave crowds spellbound. Combined with immersive aesthetic production, that becomes a tailored sensory experience rather than a booking.

Packages

Three Scales of Production

Every engagement is bespoke, but it will usually begin from one of these three.

  • The Intimate Salon

    Tier 01 · Up to 50 guests

    Single-artist performances in boutique settings.

  • The Signature Showcase

    Tier 02 · Up to 200 guests

    Multi-act or headliner private concerts with tailored production.

  • The Flagship Gala

    Tier 03 · Full production

    Full-scale bespoke event production with exclusive venue takeovers, custom set builds, luxury catering integrations and multi-day experiences.

Unbound by fixed pricing structures, our atelier approaches each project as a unique architectural undertaking. The scale of investment is shaped exclusively by your aesthetic ambition and the specific requirements needed to bring it to life. We commit fully to the meticulous realisation of every commission, maintaining a lead time of four weeks for private affairs and eight to twelve weeks for large-scale performances and multi-day retreats.

We work from Bhubaneswar and across pan-Odisha and the tier-1 metros, and are fully equipped for destination properties, heritage sites and remote locations nationwide. Travel, lodging and logistics for talent, venue operations and production crew are billed separately at actuals, or quoted as a transparent pass-through line item.

Booking terms are 50% on proposal approval to secure talent and venues, 40% two weeks before the event, and 10% on the day. Deposits are non-refundable within 30 days of the event date, because artist contracts and exclusive venue holds are locked by that point.

What we do not do

  • Mass-market standard wedding coordination, or templated social parties.
  • Dry venue-only rentals without curated talent or experiential design.
  • Off-the-shelf, low-budget talent booking, or turnkey sound equipment rental without full event management.
